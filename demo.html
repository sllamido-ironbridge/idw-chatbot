Getting there

Travel & Fees

Airport Transfers

Roundtrip from Cancún International Airport (CUN): $35 per person, automatically included. Baggage allowance: one suitcase and one carry-on per passenger. Note: your departure transfer pickup could be up to 5 hours prior to your flight.

Regional Taxes

Environmental Tax: 26 MXN (~$1.40 USD) per room, per night, paid at the resort. VISITAX: 271 MXN (~$16 USD) per person, paid via visitax.gob.mx prior to or upon departure.

Travel Insurance

Available for U.S./Canadian residents (excluding Quebec). Typical prices are $99–$199 per person. Coverage varies; the cost is non-refundable once purchased.

Cancellation Policy: All payments, including the deposit, are non-refundable unless you purchase the travel insurance option. The resort prohibits room changes within 75 days of travel and reductions after final payment.
